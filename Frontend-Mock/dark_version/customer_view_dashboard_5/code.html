quiz Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a service appointment? expand_more
You can book a service appointment directly through your dashboard by clicking on the "Book Service" button. Alternatively, you can call our support line during operating hours. We recommend booking at least 3 days in advance for routine maintenance.
What are your operating hours? expand_more
Our garage is open Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. On Saturdays, we are open for express services from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and major public holidays.
Do you offer loaner vehicles? expand_more
Yes, we offer courtesy loaner vehicles for services that require your car to be in the shop for more than 24 hours. These are subject to availability and must be reserved when booking your appointment.
How can I view and pay my invoices? expand_more
All your past and current invoices are available under the "Invoices" tab in the top navigation. You can view details, download PDFs, and pay securely online using a credit card or bank transfer.
